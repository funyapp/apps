# About LightPhotos

LightPhotos is a user-friendly and smooth-running photo clean and organization app. With minimal swipes and taps, you can easily clean up your photo album and quickly free up phone storage space. Keep your photo album clean and organized, and say goodbye to storage anxiety.

## Sortout Modes

- Random Review: Tap randomly generated photos to start organizing randomly, combining recollection and organization.
- Sequential Browsing: Organize sequentially by album or by year and month. Automatically saves your progress.

## Gesture Controls

- Swipe left to mark as saved and switch to next.
- Swipe right to switch to previous.
- Swipe up to mark as pending deletion. Then go to the `Confirm Delete` page to batch delete marked photos.
- Swipe down to favorite/unfavorite.
- Single tap to play animated or videos.
- Double tap to zoom in on images or videos.

## Photo and Video Compression

Compress photos and videos in a visually lossless manner, preserving memories while freeing up space.

- Supports multi-selection and batch compression.
- Supports various custom compression parameters.
- Supports formats: JPG, PNG, HEIC, HEIF, LivePhoto, Gif, MP4, MOV.

## Privacy and Security

We take your privacy and security very seriously.

- All photos and videos are processed locally only and are not uploaded to servers.
- We do not collect or upload any personal information or privacy data, ensuring your privacy and security.
- Deleted photos are retained in your phone's Photos app - `Recently Deleted`, and can be restored at any time within 30 days.
